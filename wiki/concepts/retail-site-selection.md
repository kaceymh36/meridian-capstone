# Retail site selection

## Definition in project context

For this interview-preparation wiki, retail site selection refers to the process of evaluating a prospective location using relevant market, customer, competitive, physical-site, financial, legal, and operational information. It is sector context only until Meridian-specific evidence is obtained. [Source record](../../raw/safegraph-retail-site-selection-checklist-2026.md)

## Sourced evidence

SafeGraph describes a site-selection process that includes mapping the store landscape; assessing customer demographics, mobility, and competitors; reviewing site access and parking; evaluating financial and legal feasibility; and validating post-opening results against forecasts. [Source record](../../raw/safegraph-retail-site-selection-checklist-2026.md)

For grocery locations, the source emphasizes tight trade areas, parking, and competitive-saturation analysis. [Source record](../../raw/safegraph-retail-site-selection-checklist-2026.md)
