# Grocery Formats and Grocery-Anchored Retail

## Definition in project context

For this interview-preparation wiki, this concept covers changes in grocery store formats, physical footprints, positioning, and the role of grocery stores as retail-center anchors. It is sector context only until Meridian-specific evidence is obtained. [Source record](../../raw/icsc-grocery-formats-store-growth-2026-04-24.md)

## Sourced evidence

ICSC describes retailers and investors adapting grocery formats, physical stores, and grocery-anchored retail strategies. [Source record](../../raw/icsc-grocery-formats-store-growth-2026-04-24.md)

JLL identifies value-focused operators and fresh-format specialty grocers as grocery categories gaining visits. It also reports overall grocery-opening concentrations in the Southeast and selected Mid-Atlantic markets, without attributing those locations specifically to specialty grocers. [Source record](../../raw/jll-grocery-tracker-2026-value-fresh-format-location.md)
