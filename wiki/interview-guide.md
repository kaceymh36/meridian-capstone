# Meridian Stakeholder Interview Guide

## Priority questions

### [Priority] Question

- Rationale: link to the relevant briefing claim or hypothesis.
- Expected evidence: state what answer, artifact, or example would be useful.
- Source context: link to the supporting raw source or write `No source context yet`.

### [High] How, if at all, do shifts in grocery formats, store footprints, or grocery-anchored retail affect Meridian's current priorities?

- Rationale: sector sources report retailers experimenting with formats and physical-store strategies, and identify visit growth in value-focused and fresh-format grocery categories; this question tests whether that context is relevant to Meridian. [ICSC source record](../raw/icsc-grocery-formats-store-growth-2026-04-24.md) [JLL source record](../raw/jll-grocery-tracker-2026-value-fresh-format-location.md)
- Expected evidence: a concrete Meridian initiative, operating constraint, customer need, or statement that the sector trend is not relevant.
- Source context: [ICSC grocery-sector source record](../raw/icsc-grocery-formats-store-growth-2026-04-24.md).

### [High] Which markets, if any, are priority for Meridian's grocery-related work, and what local demand or site factors drive that priority?

- Rationale: a sector source identifies overall grocery-opening concentrations in the Southeast and selected Mid-Atlantic markets, while distinguishing that those figures are not specialty-grocer-specific. This question tests Meridian's own market priorities rather than assuming they follow the sector pattern. [Source record](../raw/jll-grocery-tracker-2026-value-fresh-format-location.md)
- Expected evidence: Meridian's priority geographies, target customer or trade-area criteria, pipeline context, and any markets explicitly out of scope.
- Source context: [JLL grocery-tracker source record](../raw/jll-grocery-tracker-2026-value-fresh-format-location.md).

### [High] Which factors does Meridian use to evaluate a prospective retail location, and how are outcomes validated after opening?

- Rationale: a sector source describes a process that considers market, customer, competitive, physical-site, financial, legal, and post-opening performance information; this question tests whether that context is relevant to Meridian. [Source record](../raw/safegraph-retail-site-selection-checklist-2026.md)
- Expected evidence: Meridian's site-selection criteria, decision owner, supporting data, approval process, and examples of forecast-versus-actual review.
- Source context: [SafeGraph site-selection source record](../raw/safegraph-retail-site-selection-checklist-2026.md).
