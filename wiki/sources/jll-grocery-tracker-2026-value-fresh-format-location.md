## Key points

- JLL identifies value-focused operators and fresh-format specialty grocers as grocery categories gaining visits, and names Trader Joe's, Whole Foods, and Sprouts as fresh-format chains. [Source record](../../raw/jll-grocery-tracker-2026-value-fresh-format-location.md)
- JLL reports that 215 of 400 tracked grocery-store openings in 2025 were in the Southeast, with Florida leading; it also identifies clusters around New Jersey, Maryland, and the Washington, D.C./Baltimore corridor. [Source record](../../raw/jll-grocery-tracker-2026-value-fresh-format-location.md)
- The source's geographic-opening data concerns grocery expansion overall; it does not attribute those locations specifically to fresh-format specialty grocers. [Source record](../../raw/jll-grocery-tracker-2026-value-fresh-format-location.md)
