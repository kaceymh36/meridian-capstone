## Key points

- SafeGraph presents site selection as a process combining store-network mapping, demographic and mobility analysis, competition and co-tenancy, physical-site review, financial and legal review, performance analysis, and post-opening validation. [Source record](../../raw/safegraph-retail-site-selection-checklist-2026.md)
- For grocery, the article emphasizes tight trade areas, parking supply and layout, and competitive-saturation analysis. [Source record](../../raw/safegraph-retail-site-selection-checklist-2026.md)
- The article recommends comparing post-opening performance with the forecast that supported a site decision. [Source record](../../raw/safegraph-retail-site-selection-checklist-2026.md)
